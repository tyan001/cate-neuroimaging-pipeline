A real staging batch after every stage has run: ingest, recon-all + hippocampal
segmentation, SUVR prepare/register/quantify, symlink farms, study tables and the
viewer conversion. Taken from a 3-subject site-320 batch, then de-identified:

  Subject IDs   renumbered 990001-990003 (unused by real data or by testdata/)
  Dates         shifted per subject; intervals within a subject are exact
  Timestamps    scanner acquisition times and run times replaced with <ts>
  Links         Dropbox URLs in mri.csv / pet.csv removed
  Sizes         real

Output formats and log excerpts: examples/processed_batch_outputs.md
The idealised layout:            examples/adrc_tree.txt, docs/04-data-organization.md

The three Subjects cover three shapes:

  990001   MRI only. No PET, so no Pair; still gets a Subject logs/ folder.
  990002   MRI and PET on the SAME DAY, so one Session holds both
           (anat/ + freesurfer741/ + pet/ + ct/ + suvr/).
  990003   PET on 20250602, MRI three days LATER on 20250605, in separate
           Sessions. The PET pairs with an MRI that comes after it.
